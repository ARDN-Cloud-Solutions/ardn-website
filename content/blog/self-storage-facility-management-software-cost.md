<p>Self-storage management platforms are typically priced per facility, banded by unit count, rather than per user or strictly per unit. That base subscription is often the smaller piece of the real cost picture — the more consequential number for many operators is the revenue share on tenant insurance and protection plans layered on top of the core management fee.</p>

<h2>How self-storage software pricing actually works</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Per-facility subscription, banded by size.</strong> The core management platform is billed per site per month, with the facility's total unit count used to place it into a pricing band rather than billed as a strict linear per-unit charge.</li>
  <li><strong>Payment processing is a separate cost layer.</strong> Rent and fee processing typically runs through the platform's own payment rails, with its own percentage-based cost on top of the software subscription.</li>
  <li><strong>Tenant insurance and protection plans carry a revenue-share structure.</strong> Storage platforms commonly bundle tenant insurance or protection-plan offerings, splitting that revenue between the platform and the facility operator — the operator's share of that split is a real, negotiable number worth confirming directly with the vendor rather than assuming a default split.</li>
  <li><strong>Call center and online-rental portal access are often separate add-ons.</strong> A tenant-facing online move-in/rental portal and outsourced call-center coverage are frequently sold as distinct products from the core facility-management subscription, not bundled by default.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why the revenue-share layer matters more than it looks</h2>
<p>A facility comparing "software cost" across vendors by subscription price alone is missing where a meaningful share of platform revenue — and a meaningful share of what the operator could otherwise keep — actually sits. Tenant insurance is typically a mandatory or near-mandatory line item for renters, which makes the revenue split on it a recurring cost decision baked into every lease, not a one-time software line item.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is self-storage software billed per unit or per facility?</h3>
<p>Typically per facility, with the facility's total unit count used to determine which pricing band it falls into — not a strict linear per-unit charge.</p>

<h3>Does the operator keep all the revenue from tenant insurance plans?</h3>
<p>Not usually — tenant insurance and protection plans are commonly structured as a revenue share between the facility operator and the software/insurance vendor; the exact split is negotiable and worth confirming directly.</p>

<h3>Is an online tenant rental portal included in the base subscription?</h3>
<p>Not always — online move-in and rental portals, along with call-center services, are frequently sold as separate add-ons rather than bundled into the core facility-management fee.</p>
