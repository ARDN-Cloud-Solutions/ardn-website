<p>Association management systems (AMS platforms) run the core of most membership organizations — member records, dues, events, committees. Pricing models vary more than in most software categories: some AMS vendors bill per staff seat, some add module fees for events or committees, and some layer in per-member charges on top. Whichever combination applies to you, the bill tends to grow in ways that outpace your actual staff headcount.</p>

<h2>Where AMS costs actually come from</h2>

<h3>Staff seats</h3>
<p>Every staff member who touches the AMS — membership coordinators, events staff, finance — typically needs a seat, priced similarly to any per-user business software.</p>

<h3>Module add-ons</h3>
<p>Events management, committee management, learning management, and job boards are commonly sold as separate modules on top of the core AMS, each with its own fee.</p>

<h3>Member-count-based fees</h3>
<p>Some AMS platforms price partly by total member count, which means the software bill grows automatically as your association grows membership — the opposite of what you'd want a system meant to support growth to do.</p>

<h2>Who doesn't need a full AMS seat</h2>
<p>Beyond the core staff running the system daily, a lot of AMS-adjacent activity comes from people who don't need full access:</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Do we have to switch AMS platforms to fix this?</h3>
<p>No. A custom portal is built to sync with your existing AMS through its API — it's an additional front end for specific user groups, not a replacement for your core system.</p>

<h3>Can this handle chapter-specific or committee-specific permissions?</h3>
<p>Yes — a portal can be scoped so chapter leaders and committee volunteers only see data relevant to their chapter or committee, which is often harder to configure cleanly inside a general-purpose AMS permission model.</p>

<h3>Does this help with member-count-based AMS fees specifically?</h3>
<p>Not directly — those fees are typically tied to your core AMS contract terms. What a portal does address is staff-seat and module-fee growth from people who don't need full AMS access, which is usually the larger and more controllable part of the bill.</p>
