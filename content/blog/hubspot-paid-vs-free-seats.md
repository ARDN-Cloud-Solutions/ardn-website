<p>HubSpot's licensing model separates free CRM access from paid Sales, Marketing, and Service Hub seats — but a lot of orgs assign paid seats by default, department by department, without checking whether the person actually uses any paid-tier feature. That default is where a meaningful chunk of avoidable HubSpot spend lives.</p>

<h2>What a free HubSpot seat actually includes</h2>
<p>Free CRM users can view and, depending on permissions, edit standard CRM records — contacts, companies, deals — log activities, and see shared data. What they can't do is use the features gated to paid Sales Hub, Marketing Hub, or Service Hub seats: sequences, advanced reporting, forecasting, workflow automation authoring, and similar paid-tier tools.</p>

<h2>The decision test</h2>
<p>For each person currently on a paid seat, ask one question: in the last month, did they use a feature that's exclusive to the paid tier — not just log in and look at a record? If the honest answer is no, they're very likely a candidate to move to free CRM access instead.</p>

<h2>Where free seats stop being enough</h2>
<p>Free access has its own ceiling: HubSpot caps how many free users an account can have and how much customization (custom properties, permission granularity) is available without a paid tier attached somewhere in the account. For occasional internal users who just need to see or touch a record, that's usually fine. For a growing population of external users — clients, partners, field contractors — free seats aren't really designed for that use case at all, and you run into the account-level free-user cap.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Can we downgrade a paid seat to free without losing their history?</h3>
<p>Yes. The user's activity history and record ownership stay intact — downgrading changes what features they can access going forward, not what already happened in the account.</p>

<h3>Is there a limit on how many free users an account can have?</h3>
<p>Yes, HubSpot caps free user counts per account (the exact number varies by plan, so check your current agreement). That cap is one of the reasons a growing external-user population — clients, partners — is usually a better fit for a portal than for more free HubSpot seats.</p>

<h3>Do free users see the same data as paid users?</h3>
<p>They can see and interact with standard CRM data based on the permissions you set, but they can't access paid-tier tooling like sequences, advanced workflows, or custom reporting dashboards restricted to paid seats.</p>
