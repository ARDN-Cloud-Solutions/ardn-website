<p>Standalone field service platforms — the ServiceTitan/Jobber/Housecall Pro category, distinct from Salesforce's own Field Service Lightning add-on — price primarily per technician and per office/dispatcher seat. For a growing HVAC, plumbing, electrical, or home-services fleet, that mechanic means every truck added to the roster is a recurring line item, not a one-time hire cost.</p>

<h2>How dispatch software pricing scales with fleet size</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Per-technician mobile seats.</strong> Each field technician using the mobile app for job status, routing, and photo/signature capture is billed individually — full-time, part-time, and seasonal technicians alike.</li>
  <li><strong>Office/dispatcher seats priced separately.</strong> The scheduling and dispatch console typically carries its own, often higher, per-seat tier than field technician access.</li>
  <li><strong>Add-on modules for payments, marketing, and reporting.</strong> Core dispatch and scheduling is frequently the base tier; invoicing/payments processing, marketing automation, and advanced reporting are common paid add-ons.</li>
  <li><strong>Contract minimums and annual commitments.</strong> Many platforms in this category require annual contracts with a minimum seat count, making it hard to right-size down during a slow season.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why growing service fleets feel this acutely</h2>
<p>A service business that scales from 10 trucks to 40 trucks over a couple of growth seasons hasn't changed its core workflow — it's added technicians and dispatchers in direct proportion to fleet size. Because the software bills per person rather than per job or per flat organizational fee, revenue growth and software cost growth move in lockstep in a way that erodes the margin benefit of scaling up.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is this the same as Salesforce Field Service Lightning?</h3>
<p>No — this covers standalone, non-Salesforce dispatch and job-management platforms built specifically for home-services and trade businesses. Field Service Lightning is a Salesforce-native add-on with a different pricing mechanic, covered separately.</p>

<h3>Can seasonal technicians be removed to cut cost mid-year?</h3>
<p>Depends on the contract — many platforms in this category require annual commitments with seat minimums, which limits how much a business can flex down during a slow season without renegotiating.</p>

<h3>Does a custom dispatch app still handle payments and invoicing?</h3>
<p>Yes — it's built to your specific workflow, including integrating with your existing payment processor, so you're not paying for a bundled payments module you may already have solved elsewhere.</p>
