<p>HubSpot's Commerce Hub was renamed Revenue Hub, expanding its scope from billing and payments to a fuller quote-to-cash offering. The rename matters for cost planning because the product's pricing story changed along with the name: what used to be described as a pure "pay only when your customers pay you" add-on now also carries a per-seat requirement for its CPQ and quoting functionality.</p>

<h2>How Revenue Hub's pricing actually splits</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Payment collection stays transaction-metered, not seat-metered.</strong> Online payment collection, invoices, and payment links are billed on the transaction-fee structure — no seat charge to send an invoice or collect a payment.</li>
  <li><strong>Subscriptions remain a free feature.</strong> Recurring billing functionality is described as accessible to both free and premium HubSpot users, without a separate seat requirement of its own.</li>
  <li><strong>CPQ and advanced quoting now require a paid Revenue Hub seat.</strong> Creating and editing quotes, and creating change or renewal quotes, requires a Revenue Hub Professional or Enterprise seat — a real per-user meter that didn't exist under the product's original Commerce Hub framing.</li>
  <li><strong>Basic quoting may still exist outside the Revenue Hub seat requirement.</strong> HubSpot's documentation distinguishes standard "quotes" from more advanced CPQ capability; confirm directly in HubSpot's current pricing and help documentation exactly where that line sits before assuming a Sales Hub seat alone covers configurable or renewal quoting.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this is easy to get wrong in 2026</h2>
<p>A team that evaluated Commerce Hub a year or two ago and remembers it as a free, transaction-only add-on is working from a pricing model that's no longer complete. The rename to Revenue Hub came with an expanded scope — full quote-to-cash — and the new seat requirement for CPQ is the part most likely to be missing from an older cost estimate.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is Commerce Hub still HubSpot's current product name?</h3>
<p>No — HubSpot renamed it Revenue Hub, expanding it from billing/payments into a fuller quote-to-cash product; older references to "Commerce Hub" describe the prior, narrower version.</p>

<h3>Does Revenue Hub charge a seat fee just to collect payments?</h3>
<p>No — payment collection, invoicing, and payment links remain transaction-fee-based; the per-seat requirement applies specifically to CPQ and advanced quoting functionality.</p>

<h3>Are subscriptions a paid feature in Revenue Hub?</h3>
<p>No — recurring billing/subscriptions are described as available to both free and premium HubSpot users without an additional seat charge.</p>
