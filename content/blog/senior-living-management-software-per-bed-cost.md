<p>Senior living and skilled nursing platforms — PointClickCare and MatrixCare are the two names that dominate this category — license by bed count and facility, not by a flat per-organization fee. Neither vendor publishes a public rate card, so pricing is negotiated per deployment, but the shape of the bill is consistent across reported figures: a base platform fee that climbs with bed count, additional per-facility charges for multi-site operators, and a growth clause that raises the rate automatically as occupancy or bed count increases.</p>

<h2>How the per-bed model actually works</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Base fee scales with licensed beds.</strong> Third-party estimates put small-organization deployments in the low thousands per month and larger, multi-facility operators well into five figures monthly — Salesforce-style official rate cards don't exist here, so any specific figure should be treated as a reported estimate, not a quoted price.</li>
  <li><strong>Growth clauses raise the rate as the facility fills.</strong> Adding beds or opening a new wing isn't just more revenue for the operator — it's a contractual trigger that increases the software bill, often before the added capacity is fully occupied and generating revenue.</li>
  <li><strong>Per-facility charges for multi-site operators.</strong> A portfolio operator running several communities typically pays per facility, so consolidating administrative or family-communication workflows across sites doesn't reduce the per-site licensing cost.</li>
  <li><strong>Per-connection interface fees.</strong> Integrations with pharmacy, lab, or billing clearinghouse systems are frequently billed as separate per-connection fees on top of the base platform cost, and these add up quickly for a facility with several external data feeds.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Where native platforms still earn their keep</h2>
<p>Clinical documentation, MDS/care-plan compliance, and medication administration records carry real regulatory weight, and the purpose-built EHR data model behind PointClickCare or MatrixCare reflects years of skilled-nursing-specific compliance work that a custom build shouldn't try to replicate from scratch. That core clinical record is usually the wrong place to look for savings.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Does a family/admin portal replace our clinical EHR?</h3>
<p>No — it's built to sync with your existing clinical platform via API. MDS documentation, care plans, and medication records stay in the certified EHR; the portal covers the family-facing and administrative workload that doesn't need that clinical data model.</p>

<h3>Why does the software bill rise even when we haven't signed a new contract?</h3>
<p>Growth clauses in many senior living platform contracts tie the rate to current bed count or occupancy, so adding beds or opening a wing can trigger a higher bill under the existing agreement, not a renegotiation.</p>

<h3>Does this help a single-facility operator, or only multi-site portfolios?</h3>
<p>Both, but the savings compound faster for multi-site operators paying per-facility fees — a single flat-fee build replacing several per-facility charges is where the math moves the most.</p>
