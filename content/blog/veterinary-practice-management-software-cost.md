<p>Veterinary practice management platforms handle scheduling, medical records, billing, and inventory for a clinic, licensed per user across DVMs, vet techs, and front-desk staff. As independent clinics increasingly consolidate into multi-location practice groups, that per-seat model compounds the same way it does in any multi-site business — every location's staff needs its own licensed seats, and the roles that touch the system narrowly still often pay the same rate as the roles that use its full depth.</p>

<h2>Where per-seat costs add up in a veterinary practice</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Every role type licensed at similar rates.</strong> A front-desk staffer scheduling appointments and processing payment can carry a licensing cost comparable to a DVM working full medical-record depth, even though the actual feature usage is far narrower.</li>
  <li><strong>Relief and part-time veterinarians.</strong> Practices that bring in relief DVMs for coverage shifts often need to provision (and later deprovision) a full seat for coverage that may only run a few shifts a month.</li>
  <li><strong>Multi-location practice groups.</strong> As independent clinics consolidate under a shared ownership group, each location's staff typically needs its own licensed seats — consolidation doesn't reduce the underlying per-user cost, and often adds cross-location reporting tiers on top.</li>
  <li><strong>Narrow support roles.</strong> Groomers, boarding staff, or kennel technicians checking a single appointment or care instruction frequently need the same platform access as clinical staff to see it.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Where native practice management software still earns its keep</h2>
<p>Medical record accuracy, controlled-substance logging, and DVM scheduling depth are genuinely well-served by purpose-built veterinary platforms — replacing that clinical core isn't the point, and most practices are right to keep it as the system of record.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Does a staff portal replace our practice management system?</h3>
<p>No — it's built to work alongside your existing platform, syncing via API. DVMs and clinical staff keep working in the native system; the portal covers front-desk, support, and relief-coverage roles that don't need the full clinical depth.</p>

<h3>Does this help with multi-location veterinary groups specifically?</h3>
<p>Yes — a flat monthly fee means adding locations or bringing in relief coverage doesn't change the software cost the way per-seat licenses multiply per site and per engagement.</p>

<h3>Is switching practice management vendors a better fix than this?</h3>
<p>Rarely — most veterinary platforms price per user in a similar way, so switching usually changes the vendor without changing the underlying seat-cost structure.</p>
