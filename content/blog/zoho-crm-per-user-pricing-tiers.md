<p>Zoho CRM markets itself on price relative to Salesforce or HubSpot, and the entry tiers genuinely are cheaper per seat. What that comparison misses is the upsell path once a team outgrows the base CRM: Zoho doesn't just raise the per-seat rate tier by tier, it also pushes toward company-wide bundles — Zoho CRM Plus and Zoho One — that price against every employee in the organization, not just CRM users.</p>

<h2>How the per-user model actually works</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Four paid tiers plus a free edition.</strong> Reported annual-billing rates run Standard at $14/user/month, Professional at $23, Enterprise at $40, and Ultimate at $52 — roughly 3.7x from bottom to top tier — with a free edition capped at 3 users. Month-to-month billing runs noticeably higher at each tier.</li>
  <li><strong>Add-ons move the real total.</strong> Advanced automation, additional storage, and certain AI features are gated behind add-on purchases on top of the per-user tier price, and add-on costs vary enough that the sticker price alone understates the total.</li>
  <li><strong>Zoho CRM Plus bundles adjacent apps per user.</strong> Reported around $57/user/month annually, CRM Plus bundles help desk, social, and marketing tools with the core CRM — priced per CRM user, not company-wide.</li>
  <li><strong>Zoho One prices per employee, not per CRM user.</strong> At roughly $37/employee/month annually, Zoho One is licensed against total headcount across the company, regardless of how many employees actually touch the CRM — a materially different unit than any other pricing tier in this category.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Where the licensed platform still earns its keep</h2>
<p>Zoho's breadth — CRM, help desk, marketing, and finance apps under one login — is a genuine advantage for a company that wants a single vendor relationship across functions, and that integration work reflects real platform engineering. The core CRM pipeline and reporting data model is usually not where the savings opportunity lives.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is Zoho CRM actually cheaper than Salesforce or HubSpot?</h3>
<p>At the entry tiers, yes on sticker price. But the tier ladder still climbs roughly 3.7x from Standard to Ultimate, and moving to Zoho One shifts the pricing unit from CRM users to total company headcount, which can raise the total more than the per-seat comparison suggests.</p>

<h3>What's the difference between Zoho CRM Plus and Zoho One?</h3>
<p>CRM Plus bundles help desk, social, and marketing tools priced per CRM user. Zoho One bundles nearly the entire Zoho app suite but prices against every employee in the company, whether or not they use the CRM at all.</p>

<h3>Does a custom portal replace Zoho CRM?</h3>
<p>No — it's built to sync with Zoho via API. The core sales pipeline and cross-app workflows stay in Zoho; the portal covers light reporting and self-service access that doesn't need a full per-seat or per-employee license.</p>
