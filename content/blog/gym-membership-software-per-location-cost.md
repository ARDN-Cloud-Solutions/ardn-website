<p>Gym and studio management platforms typically price on two axes at once: a base fee per location, and a tier that climbs with active member count. For a single studio, that's a manageable formula. For a multi-location operator or a franchise group, the two axes compound — every new location adds its own base fee, and every location's member growth pushes its own tier independently, so the bill scales in two directions as the business succeeds.</p>

<h2>Where the per-location model gets expensive</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Base fee stacking per location.</strong> Each location typically carries its own subscription instance, so a five-location operator pays the base fee five times over rather than once for a shared account.</li>
  <li><strong>Member-count tiers per location.</strong> Growing membership at any one location can trigger a tier increase there specifically — a location's own success drives its own cost up, independent of the others.</li>
  <li><strong>Front-desk and instructor staff seats.</strong> Some platforms license front-desk check-in and instructor scheduling access as its own seat, meaning a narrow task — checking a member in, confirming a class roster — still requires a licensed login.</li>
  <li><strong>Marketing and automation add-ons.</strong> Features like automated retention campaigns or advanced reporting are frequently reserved for higher tiers, pushing an operator who wants one specific feature into a broader (and per-location-multiplied) tier jump.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Does a staff portal replace our gym management platform?</h3>
<p>No — it's built to work alongside your existing scheduling and payments platform, syncing via API. Member-facing booking and billing stay in the native system; the portal covers staff-side check-in and roster tasks across locations.</p>

<h3>Does this help specifically with multi-location or franchise costs?</h3>
<p>Yes — a flat monthly fee means adding locations or growing membership at any one site doesn't change the software cost the way per-location base fees and per-location member tiers compound.</p>
