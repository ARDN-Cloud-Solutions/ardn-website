<p>Booking platforms built for hair and nail salons — Vagaro, Boulevard, GlossGenius, Fresha, and similar — bill by staff member on the calendar, full stop. That billing unit doesn't distinguish between a W-2 employee and an independent contractor renting a chair or booth, which creates a specific cost problem in a business model where a booth renter's revenue share to the salon is typically much smaller than an employee's.</p>

<h2>How the per-staff mechanic actually works</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Per-booked-staff-member add-on pricing</strong> is the norm — a base plan for one staff member, then a per-additional-staff charge as headcount on the calendar grows, regardless of employment status.</li>
  <li><strong>Staff-count tier ceilings</strong> — some platforms tier plans by a maximum staff count rather than a strict per-head fee, which still forces a tier upgrade once a salon of any staffing model — employees or renters — crosses the ceiling.</li>
  <li><strong>Payroll add-ons priced per bookable team member</strong> — several platforms sell payroll as a separate module, again billed per person taking bookings, not per person on actual payroll.</li>
  <li><strong>No distinct booth-rental pricing tier</strong> — none of the major platforms offer a discounted rate specifically for independent-contractor renters, despite the fundamentally different revenue economics between a renter and an employee.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this is a real cost problem for booth-rental salons specifically</h2>
<p>An employee-model salon takes the majority of every service dollar and pays software cost as a percentage of a large revenue base. A booth-rental salon typically collects a flat weekly or monthly rent from each renter — a much smaller, fixed amount per chair — while the software bills the same per-head fee either way. The result: software cost as a share of what the salon actually keeps from each renter is meaningfully higher than the same math run on an employee, even though the platform treats them identically on the invoice.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Do any platforms offer a real booth-rental discount tier?</h3>
<p>Not among the major vendors as of this writing — rent-collection features exist on some platforms, but they're layered onto the same per-staff-member billing, not a separate discounted pricing structure for renters specifically.</p>

<h3>Can a custom system still collect booth rent automatically?</h3>
<p>Yes — automated rent collection from renters is a scoped feature at build time, not something exclusive to the SaaS platforms that currently offer it.</p>

<h3>Is this worth evaluating for a small, all-employee salon?</h3>
<p>Less urgently — the cost mismatch is sharpest for booth-rental and hybrid salons specifically, since that's where the fixed-rent-vs-per-head-billing gap actually shows up.</p>
