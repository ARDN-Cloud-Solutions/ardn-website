<p>Donor management platforms are priced the same way most CRMs are — per user seat, sometimes with an additional tier based on donor record count or annual revenue processed. For a small nonprofit with a lean staff, that's manageable. As the organization grows its donor base and adds seasonal event staff, volunteer coordinators, and board members who all need some level of visibility, the seat count creeps up faster than the fundraising team itself does.</p>

<h2>Who ends up on a donor-platform seat unnecessarily</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Board members and committee chairs</strong> who want visibility into fundraising progress but don't work donor records day to day.</li>
  <li><strong>Seasonal event and campaign staff</strong> brought on for a gala, an end-of-year push, or a capital campaign, then gone.</li>
  <li><strong>Volunteer coordinators</strong> who need to see specific donor or event data, not the full donor management toolset.</li>
  <li><strong>Program staff</strong> who need to log a touchpoint or update a status field occasionally, not manage the full donor lifecycle.</li>
</ul>
<p>Only the development/fundraising staff working donor records daily clearly need a full paid seat.</p>

<h2>The record-count dimension makes this worse over time</h2>
<p>Some donor platforms tier pricing partly by total donor or contact record count, not just seats. That means a successful growth campaign — more donors, more records — can push the account into a higher tier on top of whatever seat growth is happening, a similar misaligned incentive to per-active-user pricing in other CRM categories: the fundraising program working is what triggers the cost increase.</p>

<h2>Where a portal fits without replacing the donor platform</h2>
<p>The donor database stays the system of record for gift history, tax receipting, and compliance reporting — none of that changes. What can move is the access layer for everyone whose need is narrower than a full seat: a board dashboard showing campaign progress, a volunteer view scoped to one event, a seasonal staff view for a single campaign. A <a href="https://ardncloudsolutions.com/custom-portal-development">custom portal</a> synced to the donor platform via API delivers exactly that slice, on one flat fee regardless of how many board members or seasonal staff use it.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Does a portal put donor data at risk of being disconnected from the system of record?</h3>
<p>No — it's built to sync with your existing donor platform via API, not to replace it. Gift records, receipting, and compliance data stay exactly where they are.</p>

<h3>Is this worth it for a small nonprofit with only a few staff?</h3>
<p>The case is strongest once board visibility, volunteer access, or seasonal campaign staffing are adding seats beyond your core development team — a very small, stable org may not see much benefit yet.</p>

<h3>Can a portal handle event-specific volunteer access?</h3>
<p>Yes, when scoped to the specific event and data volunteers need during the build — that's a requirements decision made per organization, not a fixed limitation.</p>
