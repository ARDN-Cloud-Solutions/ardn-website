The patchwork

Club technology was assembled one club at a time. Multi-club operators inherit the result.

Every hand-off between systems is a place where data is re-keyed, prices drift, prospects fall through the cracks, and the controller spends the first week of every month reconciling.

Tee sheet & POS

From one vendor, with its own member list to keep in step.

Back office

From another, often a different product generation.

Websites

An agency per club, with prices that drift from the back office.

Sales & contracts

A separate CRM and a separate e-signature tool.

The portfolio

Reciprocal access in a spreadsheet; a nightly export to accounting.

One flow

From first inquiry to first round, on one member record.

The price on the website is the price in the back office

Promotions, director-sold plans and online plans are read from one price book by the website, the join flow and the Director's product builder.

Nobody pays before they sign

The contract is signed against the exact version shown, and the server refuses payment until it is, online and in a Director's in-person checkout.

A benefit is used up when it is used

Booking a tee time draws down the allowance the member saw while booking, across every club they can play.

A lead cannot be lost

Unclaimed inquiries escalate, idle deals return to the pool, nurtures come back on their date, and guest rounds turn into leads.

Control & switching without risk

Everyone sees exactly their slice, and nothing switches on a guess.

Isolation enforced in the database

Row-level security on every table: a club's staff cannot query another club's records.

A permission for every capability

400+ permissions per role and per club, per-person grants with an expiry and a reason.

Nothing important can vanish

Field history, an audit log of staff actions, and a 30-day recycle bin. Members and anything with money or signatures behind it can never be deleted.

Nothing switches all at once

Modules turn on per club, so each club moves at the pace you set while the rest carry on as today.

No club goes live on a guess

A readiness checklist, item by item. Defaults are previewed first and never overwrite what a club has customised.

Search rankings carry over

Permanent redirects from each club's old web addresses keep the traffic your sites have earned.
