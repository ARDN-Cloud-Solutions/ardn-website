Situation
The Y runs 24 capabilities across about seventeen vendors, with Daxko underneath membership, programs, reports, screening, PT packages, child watch and licensed childcare.
Complication
Daxko cannot simply be switched off. One thing it does — licensed childcare — has no equivalent in any product Ardn owns, and we verified that rather than assumed it. Meanwhile DocuSign expires at the end of April regardless.
Question
What can the Y retire now, what must be built first, and in what order — with no gap at a front desk or on a childcare licence?
Recommendation
Retire 7 outright and connect 7 more, starting with DocuSign because it has a date. Put licensed childcare on the critical path from day one, parallel to the ports. Treat SGA as a connection now, a decision later.

16 of 24 carry no build risk.

Rollout Plan

Two fixed dates: DocuSign in April, off Daxko entirely by the end of June

6months to DocuSign
8months to the Daxko cutover
4on the critical path
Two dates are fixed, and neither is ours. DocuSign ends end of April 2027 (month 6). The Y comes off Daxko entirely — end of June, beginning of July 2027 (month 8). Everything before each one is sized to clear it, and nothing is switched off until its parallel run has finished.
Licensed childcare is started first, and it is the risk. Leaving all of Daxko means the childcare build cannot wait its turn: it is the one capability with nothing to build on, so it begins in month one and gets a five-month build rather than eight. That compression is the largest risk here, and we would rather name it now than discover it in May.

Gap · Day 1
Licensed childcare and preschool

Parent sign in and out, staff-to-child ratios, incidents, health records, ELC reimbursement. Zero matches in either product — we checked each concept by name. Largest build, started first, and the July Daxko exit rests on it. Five months of build rather than eight.

Gap · Day 1
Migration off Daxko

Members, families, barcodes, stored cards, history. No import tooling exists; it needs Daxko export access and an NDA before it can be designed. Cards move processor-to-processor, never through us.

Partial · Day 1
Online program and camp registration

Programs and Classes work. A parent cannot yet register a child online, early-bird is not charged, and waivers are not enforced.

Not Day 1
Sports leagues

TeamSideline cannot integrate and nothing covers leagues. Run it alongside for one season and build against a real league in flight.

Blocked On A Vendor
Welld insurance feed

A daily member-usage file. Waiting on Welld's format, not on engineering effort.

Buy, Not Build
Sex-offender registry data

The screening workflow and audit trail already exist. The national registry data source does not, in either product.

Real Database, Not Mocks

The Y's suite runs against a live Postgres with the same roles and row-level-security policies production uses, so a permission bug fails in the pipeline instead of at a front desk.

A Shared Oracle

In the accounting engine the SQL rules and the TypeScript rules run the same cases and must agree. That found a defect where a month-end statement could never post — before a customer met it.

Nothing Merges On A Promise

CI runs the typecheck, the build and the suite on every pull request. Before it existed, “the tests pass” was a claim checked once on a laptop. Now it is a gate.

One date is fixed, and it is not ours. DocuSign ends at the end of April 2027. Wave 1 is sized backwards from it, which is why first go-live is April and not later. Everything after that is sequenced by what blocks what.
